INSIDE

What's inside.

Four sections, A to D, plus chorus, phaser and two LFOs. VIEW (right) shows how long the reverb rings at every frequency, and you drag the dots to reshape it. New in 1.14: a dice button for fresh ideas, with padlocks to keep the sections you like. New to the sound? Read how to get the 80s reverb sound.

A

PALM / REVERB

The wide, shimmering digital reverb of late-80s records. Goes from a tight plate to a twenty-second wash, with a gate for the classic 80s drum sound that you can watch open and close.

Size · Decay · Damping · Shimmer · Pre-delay · Width · Amount · Duck · Gate: threshold, hold, release

B

AGE / VHS

Puts the sound on an old tape: wobbling pitch, flutter, soft saturation, hiss, dropouts and a narrowing tone. The hiss comes in three kinds: tape, VHS, or the static, buzz and whine of an old TV set. Age everything, or only the reverb tail.

Wow · Flutter · Warmth · Hiss · Fade · Output · Hiss: Tape / VHS / TV

C

DRIFT / AUTO PAN

Moves the sound between the speakers, free or locked to your song’s tempo. Pan everything, or keep the dry sound centred while only the reverb drifts.

Rate · Depth · Sync · Sine / Triangle / Square / Random

D

MASTER

The big Nostalgia knob pushes everything towards a lush, faraway memory. Plus the mixing tools: tail EQ, tempo-synced pre-delay, sidechain ducking and a Mix knob that blends the whole effect with your dry sound.

Nostalgia · Low Cut · High Cut · Pre-delay Sync · Duck Key · Mix

+

CHORUS / PHASER

One button opens the lush section: a chorus with three characters and a slow, liquid phaser, on everything or only the reverb. The faceplate glows in time with it.

Chorus · Dimension · Ensemble · Rate · Depth · Feedback · Mix

~

LFO / SPECTRUM

Two LFOs that can move almost any knob. Drag a glowing cable onto a knob to connect it, and that knob shows a ring that moves with it. Pick from 17 shapes, from a sidechain pump to a trance gate, or click the screen and draw your own. A live spectrum shows your sound and the reverb side by side.

Drag to a knob · 17 shapes · Draw your own · Tempo sync · Depth · Spectrum

PLAYING WITH IT

  • RewindHold the button and the tape brakes, then spins backwards with that VHS squeal. Let go and it drops straight back in. Automate it to hit on the beat.
  • Roll the diceOne click rolls fresh, musical settings. Shift-click nudges what you have. Lock any section with its padlock and it stays put, through dice rolls and preset changes.
  • Built-in helpPoint at any control and the help bar says what it does. Right-click any knob for the full story, an exact value, your own range and MIDI learn.

MIXING

  • DuckingBig reverbs that step aside while the vocal sings, from the input or a sidechain.
  • A/B compare and undoTwo settings side by side, Copy A>B, and full undo and redo.
  • Switch sections offOnly want the auto-pan? Turn A and B off and they leave your sound untouched.

FAQ

Questions.

What is NightRoll?

A free piano roll plug-in by NightFin, for anyone, with or without Palm Life. Put it on a MIDI track, draw your notes, then drag the Drag MIDI button onto any track to get a normal MIDI clip. To play an instrument live, put NightRoll on its own MIDI track; on your instrument's track set MIDI From to that track, pick NightRoll in the box under it and set Monitor to In. It snaps to a key and scale, moves your notes when you change key, and has a loop selector, velocity lane and slice tool. Download it here.

What is Orbit?

A free 3D panner for everyone, even if you never buy Palm Life. It comes in the Palm Life download (get it here): run the installer, leave Orbit ticked, and add Orbit to a track in your DAW. It needs no licence key and keeps working after the trial ends. On headphones it moves a sound round your head, in front, behind and above, and can follow your song's tempo. It uses a recorded dummy head by default; you can load your own head measurement (.sofa) and a correction for your headphones (an AutoEq profile).

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Download Palm Life and you get the full plug-in for 14 days, with no card and no account. After that it keeps working but drops out for a moment every 45 seconds until you enter a licence key.

Which DAWs work?

Any DAW that loads VST3 or, on a Mac, Audio Units: Logic Pro, GarageBand, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One and Bitwig. There’s also a standalone app if you just want to play through it.

How do I install it?

Unzip the download and double-click Palm Life Setup (Windows) or Palm Life Installer (Mac). It puts the plug-in in the right folders for you. If Windows shows a warning, choose More info, then Run anyway. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW, then click REGISTER and paste your key.

Are updates free?

Yes, every 1.x update. Palm Life shows an UPDATE button when one is out: click UPDATE NOW, allow the Windows prompt and restart your DAW. Your licence and presets carry over.

Is there a Mac version?

Yes. The same licence works on Windows and Mac. Double-click Palm Life Installer. If your Mac says it can’t verify the developer, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

Moving to a new computer?

In Palm Life click LICENCE, then DEACTIVATE, and register on the new one. You can also free up computers from your customer portal.

Something not working?

Tell NightFin with the support form: what happened, your computer and DAW, and a screenshot if you can. Problems, questions and ideas are all welcome.

Can I get a refund?

Full refund within 14 days if Palm Life doesn’t work on your system. Email palmlifesupport@gmail.com. Refunded licence keys are deactivated.
